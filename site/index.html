A FEW THINGS TO KNOW
What stays on my computer?

Your shelf, notes, recordings, selected sources, and recommendations. Credentials are encrypted using the system credential store. Selected text goes directly to OpenAI when you request recommendations; catalogs receive search terms and cover requests. The library itself is not an encrypted vault. Read the exact data flow.

Does ChatGPT need a separate payment?

Aftertaste has no subscription or developer API-key billing. Eligible requests use your connected ChatGPT plan, within its access rules and limits. Manage plan access and any enabled credits in ChatGPT settings. Availability varies by account and workspace; this is not a promise of unlimited or universally free AI.

Can it read all my chats automatically?

No. Sign-in does not reveal chat history or account memory. You can import chosen conversations from your own ChatGPT data export, or selected text / Markdown documents. Nothing is collected in the background.

What can I save?

Books, manga, Steam games, and covers from live catalogs. Videos, films, podcasts, and articles by link or manual entry. Written thoughts and local voice recordings. This release does not transcribe voice, sync across devices, or import PDF / DOCX files.

Can I help build it?

Please do. Aftertaste is MIT-licensed and open source. Code, issues, documentation, and the public interface are in English. Find us on GitHub.
